Omelette Recipe

Home



Description.

An omelette is a quick and versatile egg dish, cooked in a pan and filled with ingredients like cheese, vegetables, or meats. It’s perfect for breakfast or a light meal, and ready in just minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 - 3 eggs
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Butter or oil
  • Cheese (optional)
  • Chopped vegetables (e.g., onions, bell peppers, spinach)
  • Cooked meat (optional, e.g., ham or bacon)

Preparation Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add salt and pepper, and beat until well mixed.
  2. Heat butter or oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
  3. Pour in the eggs and swirl the pan to spread them evenly.
  4. Cook for 1–2 minutes until the eggs start to set.
  5. Add cheese, vegetables, or meats on one half of the omelette.
  6. Fold the omelette in half and cook for another minute until fully set.
  7. Slide onto a plate and serve immediately.
