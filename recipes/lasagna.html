Lasagna Recipe

Home



Description.

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of tender pasta sheets, rich meat or vegetable sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese, all baked to golden perfection. Hearty, flavorful, and comforting, it’s a timeless favorite for family meals and gatherings.

Ingredients.

  • Ground beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Egg
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps.

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions (if not using oven-ready noodles).
  3. In a pan, sauté chopped onion and garlic in olive oil.
  4. Add ground beef and cook until browned. Drain excess fat.
  5. Stir in tomato sauce, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  6. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese with an egg and a bit of parmesan.
  7. In a baking dish, layer sauce, noodles, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella cheese.
  8. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, ending with sauce and cheese on top.
  9. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  10. Remove foil and bake for another 10–15 minutes until cheese is bubbly and golden.
  11. Let it rest for 10 minutes before serving.
